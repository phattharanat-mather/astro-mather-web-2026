## How We Work

We follow a collaborative, iterative process — starting with discovery, moving through design and development, and finishing with deployment and support.

### Phases

1. **Discovery** — Understand your goals, constraints, and users
2. **Design** — Wireframes, prototypes, and design systems
3. **Build** — Agile sprints with continuous delivery
4. **Launch** — Deployment, monitoring, and handoff
5. **Grow** — Ongoing support and feature evolution
