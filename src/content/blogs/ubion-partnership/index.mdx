The Mather is proud to announce a strategic business partnership with Ubion, South Korea's foremost EdTech and Artificial Intelligence company.

This collaboration marks a significant milestone in our mission to bring cutting-edge AI and data science solutions to Thailand and the broader Southeast Asian market.

## About the Partnership

Ubion brings decades of expertise in educational technology and AI-driven learning platforms. Together with The Mather's deep roots in data science, AI implementation, and regional market knowledge, this partnership is positioned to deliver transformative solutions for businesses and educational institutions alike.

## What This Means

- Joint development of AI-powered learning and analytics platforms
- Knowledge exchange between Korean AI expertise and Thai market insights
- Expanded service offerings for enterprise clients across SEA

We look forward to sharing more about our joint projects in the coming months.
