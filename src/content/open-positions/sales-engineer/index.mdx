## About the Role

We are looking for a Sales Engineer to join our Business Development team. You will be the primary technical face in client engagements — leading pre-sales meetings, running requirement-gathering sessions, and owning the onboarding process that sets every project up for success. This role sits at the intersection of consulting, communication, and technical fluency.

## What You Will Do

- Lead client discovery meetings and requirement-gathering workshops
- Translate client business problems into clear technical scopes for internal teams
- Prepare and present solution proposals, demos, and proof-of-concept walkthroughs
- Own client onboarding: coordinate handoffs between sales and delivery, set expectations, and ensure a smooth project start
- Maintain relationships with key stakeholders throughout the engagement lifecycle
- Collaborate with data, engineering, and design teams to validate feasibility before commitments are made
- Feed client insights back into the product and service roadmap

## What We Are Looking For

- 3+ years in a technical consulting, pre-sales, or solutions engineering role
- Strong communication skills in Thai and English — comfortable presenting to C-level stakeholders
- Solid understanding of software development, data, and AI concepts (you do not need to code, but you need to understand what the team builds)
- Structured thinking: able to decompose a vague business problem into a concrete project scope
- Proven track record of running client meetings and producing requirement documents or SOWs
- High ownership mindset — you follow through without being chased

## Nice to Have

- Background in data analytics, AI products, or enterprise software
- Experience in a consulting or agency environment serving mid-to-large enterprises
- Familiarity with project management tools (Notion, Jira, or similar)

## What We Offer

- Competitive salary and performance bonus
- Hybrid working — Bangkok office + remote days
- Health insurance and annual leave
- Direct exposure to a wide range of industries and technical domains
- Small, senior team where your work shapes how clients experience The Mather
