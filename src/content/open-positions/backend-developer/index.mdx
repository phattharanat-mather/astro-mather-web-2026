## About the Role

We are looking for a Backend Developer to join our Engineering team. You will design, build, and maintain the server-side systems that underpin our AI and data platform products — from RESTful APIs to real-time data pipelines.

## What You Will Do

- Design and implement RESTful and GraphQL APIs consumed by web and mobile clients
- Build and optimise data ingestion pipelines (batch and streaming)
- Collaborate with data scientists to productionise ML models and inference endpoints
- Maintain database schemas across PostgreSQL and NoSQL stores
- Write thorough tests and participate in code review
- Monitor production services and respond to incidents

## What We Are Looking For

- 3+ years of professional backend development experience
- Strong proficiency in Python (FastAPI / Django) or Node.js
- Solid understanding of SQL and experience with PostgreSQL
- Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- Experience with Docker and basic Kubernetes knowledge
- Good communication skills and comfort working in an agile team

## Nice to Have

- Experience integrating with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.)
- Background in data engineering (Airflow, dbt, Spark)
- Prior work in a consulting or agency environment

## What We Offer

- Competitive salary and performance bonus
- Hybrid working — Bangkok office + remote days
- Health insurance and annual leave
- Learning budget for conferences and certifications
- Small, senior team with real ownership over the systems you build
