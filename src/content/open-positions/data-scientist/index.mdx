## About the Role

We are looking for a Data Scientist to join our Data & AI team. You will work across multiple client projects, applying statistical modelling, machine learning, and data analysis to solve complex business problems in industries such as finance, retail, and logistics.

## What You Will Do

- Conduct exploratory data analysis and communicate findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Build, evaluate, and iterate on machine learning models (classification, regression, clustering, forecasting)
- Design experiments and A/B tests; measure and report on outcomes
- Collaborate with backend engineers to productionise models via REST APIs or batch scoring pipelines
- Develop internal tools and reusable components for the data science practice
- Stay current with research and bring relevant techniques into client work

## What We Are Looking For

- 2+ years of hands-on data science or ML engineering experience
- Strong Python skills — pandas, scikit-learn, and at least one deep learning framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow)
- Solid foundation in statistics and probability
- Experience with SQL and working with large datasets
- Ability to explain complex models clearly in business terms
- Comfortable with version control (git) and reproducible research practices

## Nice to Have

- Experience fine-tuning or prompting LLMs for domain-specific tasks
- Familiarity with MLflow, Weights & Biases, or similar experiment-tracking tools
- Background in NLP or time-series forecasting
- Thai language skills (client-facing work)

## What We Offer

- Competitive salary and performance bonus
- Hybrid working — Bangkok office + remote days
- Health insurance and annual leave
- Learning budget for conferences and certifications
- Exposure to diverse industries and datasets across a growing client base
