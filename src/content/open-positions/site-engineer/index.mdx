## About the Role

We are looking for a Site Engineer to join our Engineering team on a contract basis. You will be the technical lead on the ground at client sites across Thailand — responsible for the end-to-end setup of systems and equipment, from unpacking and configuration through to testing and client handover.

## What You Will Do

- Unbox, inspect, and inventory hardware including computers, peripherals, networking devices, servers, and AV equipment
- Install and configure operating systems, drivers, software packages, and network settings according to project specifications
- Set up workstations, displays, network infrastructure, and related systems at client and office locations
- Apply asset tags and maintain accurate asset tracking records
- Conduct pre-installation site surveys and document technical requirements
- Perform system commissioning, functional testing, and user acceptance testing (UAT)
- Produce clear site reports, as-built documentation, and handover checklists
- Troubleshoot hardware, network, and software issues during and after deployment
- Coordinate with project managers and delivery teams to align site work with project timelines
- Travel to client sites across Bangkok and different provinces as required

## What We Are Looking For

- 2+ years of experience in a site engineering, field engineering, IT support, or technical deployment role
- Solid understanding of IT infrastructure: networking, servers, cabling, and related hardware
- Comfortable with Windows and macOS setup and configuration
- Ability to read technical diagrams and follow detailed installation specifications
- Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to work independently under time pressure
- Physically fit for lifting and moving equipment
- Good communication skills in Thai; working English for documentation
- Valid driver's licence and willingness to travel to different provinces

## Nice to Have

- Experience in data centres, smart building systems, or enterprise IT rollouts
- Familiarity with MDM tools (e.g. Jamf, Intune) or structured cabling standards
- CCNA, CompTIA Network+, or similar certification

## What We Offer

- Competitive contract rate
- Project-based engagement with potential for extended or repeat contracts
- Clear setup guides and specifications provided before every deployment
- Exposure to a range of enterprise and government client environments across Thailand
