The AI landscape moves fast. Here are five tools that have earned a place in serious business workflows in 2025.

## 1. Large Language Models (LLMs) for Internal Knowledge

LLMs like GPT-4 and Claude are being deployed internally to answer employee questions, summarize documents, and draft communications — reducing information retrieval time significantly.

## 2. AutoML Platforms

Platforms like Google Vertex AI and Amazon SageMaker Autopilot now allow non-specialists to build, train, and deploy machine learning models with minimal code.

## 3. AI-Powered BI Tools

Tools like Tableau with Einstein AI and Power BI Copilot are enabling business analysts to query data in natural language and surface insights automatically.

## 4. Computer Vision APIs

Cloud-based vision APIs have made it viable for any size business to add image recognition, quality inspection, and document processing to their workflows.

## 5. AI Governance Platforms

As AI adoption matures, so does the need for explainability and auditability. Tools in this space help organizations track model decisions, detect bias, and maintain compliance.
