Every AI initiative rests on a foundation of data. And the quality of that foundation depends on governance.

## What Is Data Governance?

Data governance is the framework that ensures data is accurate, consistent, secure, and used responsibly across an organization. It encompasses:

- **Data ownership** — who is accountable for each dataset
- **Data quality** — standards for accuracy, completeness, and timeliness
- **Data lineage** — understanding where data comes from and how it transforms
- **Access control** — who can see, edit, or use which data
- **Compliance** — meeting regulatory requirements (PDPA, GDPR, etc.)

## Why It Matters for AI

AI models are only as good as the data they're trained on. Poor governance leads to:

- Biased models trained on unrepresentative samples
- Models that fail silently because input data has drifted
- Compliance violations when sensitive data is used incorrectly
- Inability to audit or explain model decisions

## Building a Governance Foundation

Start with a data catalog — a clear inventory of what data you have, where it lives, and who owns it. From there, establish quality metrics and review cycles before any AI model goes into production.
